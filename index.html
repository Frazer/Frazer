Inner practice. Real-world possibility.

Train the mind.
Bring it into life.

The aim of my teaching is freedom from unnecessary suffering and more room for joy, skill, and connection. We explore what it feels like to relax deeply, then practise taking that ease into the moments that matter.

01

Notice what is actually here.

Observe sensations, thoughts, and habits with curiosity. Question assumptions. Stay willing to change your mind.

02

Rehearse the person you want to be.

Use visualization and self-directed suggestion to practise confidence, kindness, and the next useful action.

03

Make the practice lived experience.

Bring attention to your craft, patience to a conversation, and deliberate practice to sport, art, and learning.

My compass: compassion and reality. I want our beliefs to answer to evidence, not superstition; our aspirations to become action; and our practices to be judged by how they help us live. Experiences vary—bliss and lasting change are possibilities to explore, not guaranteed outcomes.
